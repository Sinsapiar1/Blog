Privacy Policy — PivetTrack

Responsible for this policy: PIVET DIGITAL AUTOMATIONS LLC ("Pivet", "we", "us"), a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Florida, United States. Version: 1.2 — November 4, 2026.

1. Scope

This policy explains what personal data Pivet processes in connection with:

  • PivetTrack, the inventory and warehouse management system (WMS) that Pivet provides as a service (SaaS) to businesses ("Clients");
  • the PivetTrack B2B Ordering Portal, which a Client can enable so that its own buyers can send it orders;
  • the PivetTrack B2B API, through which the systems of a Client's buyers integrate with the Client's instance; and
  • the public website www.raulpivet.com/pivettrack.html.

PivetTrack is contracted strictly business to business. This policy does not govern the relationship between a Client and its own buyers, employees or suppliers: that relationship is governed by the Client's own privacy policy.

2. Our two roles: controller and processor

Pivet processes personal data in two different capacities, and the difference matters.

As a processor. Everything that lives inside a Client's PivetTrack instance —its internal users' accounts, its buyers' B2B Portal accounts, the contacts of its customers and suppliers, its orders and its inventory movements— is processed by Pivet on behalf of, and on the instructions of, the Client, who is the controller of that data. This is the same approach taken in the PivetTrack Service Level Agreement, which defines Pivet as the technical custodian (data processor) of the Client's data.

The B2B Portal, expressly. The people who use the B2B Ordering Portal are not Pivet's customers: they are our Clients' buyers. Pivet does not choose them, does not contact them on its own behalf and does not use their data for any purpose of its own. The Client decides who gets access, is responsible for informing its buyers about the processing of their data and for having a legal basis for it, and is the recipient of any requests those individuals wish to make (Section 10). Pivet acts there solely as a processor.

As a controller. Pivet decides on its own about a limited set of data: that of visitors to the www.raulpivet.com/pivettrack.html website, that of people who write to us to learn about the product, and the contact and billing details of our Clients' representatives, which we use to manage the business relationship.

3. Data we process and why

3.1. PivetTrack internal users (the Client's staff)

  • Account data: username, first and last name, email address, role (Super Administrator, Administrator or Operator), preferred language, who created the account, creation date and date of last login.
  • Password: only its hash is stored; the plain-text password is never stored.
  • Second authentication factor: mandatory for the Super Administrator and the Administrator, optional for the Operator. The authenticator secret is stored encrypted. Single-use backup codes are also generated.

Purpose: to identify each user, to control which functions they can access according to their role, and to protect access to the account.

3.2. B2B Ordering Portal buyers (on behalf of the Client)

  • Account data: email address (which is their login identifier), name, preferred language, the Client the account belongs to, creation date, date of last login and the internal user who created the account.
  • Password: only its hash. The account is created without a usable password and the person sets it from a link they receive by email.
  • Second authentication factor: optional for each account, unless the Client requires it for all of theirs. The authenticator secret is stored encrypted. Single-use backup codes are also generated.
  • Orders: each order the person submits or cancels is attributed to them.

Purpose: to let the buyer send and track their orders to the Client, and to let the Client know who submitted each one.

3.3. Business contacts recorded by the Client

Within its instance, the Client may record contact details of its own customers and suppliers (legal name, tax identification number —EIN—, contact name, phone number, email address and postal address) and of the authorized recipients of transfers between warehouses (name, email address and phone number). Pivet does not enter or verify this data: the Client does.

Purpose: the Client's logistics operations (shipments, receipts, shipping documents).

3.4. Operational traceability

Every operational document and every inventory movement records the user who performed it and the date and time. The movement ledger (Kardex) is immutable by design: once written, it is neither modified nor deleted. Sensitive administrative actions —creating, editing, activating and deactivating users; resetting passwords and the second factor; issuing and revoking API credentials; registering and revoking webhooks— are also recorded in an audit log with the user who acted, the action, a detail and the IP address it was performed from.

Purpose: the integrity and auditability of the Client's accounting and inventory records, and establishing who performed each action.

3.5. Security and abuse prevention

  • IP address: the application obtains the visitor's real IP address from the header written by the infrastructure's edge proxy. It uses it to limit login attempts and requests (rate limiting), to temporarily block brute-force attacks, and for the audit log.
  • Internal panel access log: for each login attempt, successful or not, the username, IP address, browser user agent, path, and date and time are recorded. After five failed attempts, access is temporarily locked (30 minutes, under the default configuration).
  • B2B Portal: failed attempts are counted per combination of email address and IP address; the counter is stored under a fingerprint (SHA‑256 hash) of that combination and expires with its time window.

Purpose: to protect accounts and the platform against unauthorized access.

3.6. Integrations: B2B API and webhooks

  • API credentials: a public identifier (client ID), the secret only as a hash (it is shown once, when issued) and, if one is assigned, the fingerprint of the client certificate.
  • Webhooks: the URL of the buyer's system that receives notifications and the signing secret, which is stored encrypted because it must be readable to sign each delivery.
  • Content: the order exactly as the buyer's system sent it, and the content of each notification sent.

Purpose: to authenticate integrations, record the orders received and notify their status changes.

3.7. Transactional emails

PivetTrack sends operational emails, never marketing ones: the link to set or reset a password (Portal and internal panel), the receipt acknowledgment for a Portal order, status changes of a B2B order (approved, rejected, cancelled, shipped), the notice of a confirmed inventory count, and internal notices about API credentials about to expire. The recipient's email address and name are used to send them.

3.8. Generated and uploaded files

  • Exports: large Excel files generated in the background are stored in the instance's database so that the person who requested them can download them. They expire after 24 hours and are deleted with the next export requested in the instance. Small exports are generated on the spot and are not stored.
  • Imports: an Excel file uploaded to import products or a count is stored while it is processed, and its content is emptied when processing finishes; the file name and the result are kept.

3.9. The www.raulpivet.com/pivettrack.html website and business contact

  • Analytics: if the website uses Google Analytics (the gtag.js tag), it collects browsing data such as pages visited, device and approximate location. We configure it with a data retention of fourteen (14) months and with Google Signals disabled.
  • Contact: if you write to us, we use your name, email address, company and the content of your message to reply and, where relevant, to prepare a commercial proposal.
  • Business relationship: for our Clients' representatives we process name, title, email address and billing details. Payments are received by bank transfer (ACH or wire) or through Stripe, which processes card data under its own privacy policy: Pivet does not receive or store card numbers, and PivetTrack has no payments module.

3.10. QuickBooks Online integration (Intuit)

If the Client enables it, PivetTrack sends to the Client's own QuickBooks Online company an Invoice for each confirmed Outbound shipment and a Bill for each confirmed supplier Receipt. The connection is authorized by one of the Client's Super Administrators with their Intuit account (OAuth 2.0), and can be revoked at any time from the same screen or from the Intuit account.

  • What we store about the connection: the QuickBooks company identifier (realm ID), its name, the environment (sandbox or production), the chosen income and expense accounts, who connected it and when, and Intuit's access tokens, which are stored encrypted because they must be readable for each submission. We do not store the Intuit password: it never goes through PivetTrack.
  • What we read from QuickBooks: only what the integration needs: the company name, the list of active accounts (to choose the default accounts), and existing customers, vendors and products with the same name (to avoid duplicates). The only permission we request from Intuit is accounting (com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting).
  • What we send to QuickBooks: for customers and vendors, the legal name, email address, phone and address recorded in PivetTrack; for products, the code (SKU) and description; and for each document, its number, date, quantities, prices or costs, and the supplier's reference. We also store in the instance the identifier QuickBooks assigns to each of them, so as not to create it again.
  • Purpose: solely to record the Client's own inventory transactions in the Client's accounting. We do not use QuickBooks data for any other purpose, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties.
  • When disconnecting: PivetTrack asks Intuit to revoke the authorization and deletes the tokens. The record of which document was sent and under which number is kept, so the connection can be resumed without duplicating invoices. Data already in QuickBooks stays in the Client's account, under Intuit's terms and privacy policy.

Intuit is not a Pivet sub-processor: it is a provider the Client contracts with and chooses to connect. That is why it is not listed in the table in Section 6.

3.11. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not use the data in our Clients' instances for advertising or for any purpose of our own.
  • The PivetTrack application (internal panel and B2B Portal) loads no third-party scripts or analytics tools: its content security policy only allows resources served by the instance itself.

4. Legal bases and applicable law

We process data in accordance with the applicable laws of the United States and of the State of Florida, including the security breach notification duty set out in the Florida Information Protection Act. This policy does not claim compliance with any other particular law; if a Client needs additional commitments because of the laws that apply to it, we agree on them in writing (Section 14).

In general terms, we process data to provide the service contracted by the Client, to protect the security of the platform and, for the website and business contact, based on our interest in answering inquiries and measuring use of the site.

5. Where data is hosted: one dedicated instance per Client

PivetTrack is single-tenant: each Client receives its own isolated instance, made up of its own project on Railway (where the application runs) and its own project on Supabase (PostgreSQL database), in accounts in Pivet's name. A Client's data does not share a database with any other Client's: isolation between Clients is at the infrastructure level.

  • Location: in the United States. The application runs in Railway's U.S. East region and the database in a Supabase U.S. region.
  • Encryption in transit: the application is served only over HTTPS (it redirects HTTP and sends the HSTS header).
  • Encryption at rest: the application itself encrypts the secrets it must be able to read (Section 9). Encryption of the database storage depends on Supabase, as described in its current security documentation, and is not a commitment of Pivet's own.
  • Backups: each instance's database has an automatic daily backup, retained for seven (7) days.

7. Cookies and local storage

In the PivetTrack application (internal panel and B2B Portal), only strictly necessary cookies are used:

Name Purpose Duration
sessionid Keeping the session signed in Expires after 30 minutes of inactivity (default configuration)
csrftoken Protection against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) About one year
django_language Remembering the language chosen in the internal panel Until the browser is closed

The internal panel also stores in the browser's local storage the time of last activity, so that inactivity logout works consistently across tabs, and whether the sidebar is open or closed. It contains no personal data.

On the www.raulpivet.com/pivettrack.html website, if it uses Google Analytics, Google sets its own cookies (for example, _ga), whose duration Google defines in its documentation.

8. How long we keep data

  • Client instance data: while the contract is in force. On termination, it remains available for export for thirty (30) calendar days, under the terms of the Service Level Agreement, after which the instance and its database are deleted. When the project is deleted, Supabase also deletes its backups.
  • User accounts: they are not deleted, they are deactivated. Accounts remain linked to the movements, orders and audit records they produced, which are immutable by design; their data is deleted together with the instance.
  • Sessions: they expire through inactivity; a scheduled task deletes expired ones once a day.
  • Internal panel access log and audit log: kept while the contract is in force and deleted together with the instance.
  • Brute-force and rate-limit counters: they expire on their own, with their time window.
  • Exports: 24 hours (Section 3.8).
  • Infrastructure service logs: Railway keeps them for seven (7) days.
  • Website and business contact: Google Analytics data, fourteen (14) months; business contact emails, two (2) years from the last exchange.

9. Security

Among other measures: passwords stored only as hashes; a mandatory second factor for administrative roles; second-factor and webhook secrets encrypted in the database; API secrets stored only as hashes; login attempt limits and temporary lockout against brute force; inactivity logout; HTTPS-only cookies; a restrictive content security policy; role-based access control; and an immutable record of movements and administrative actions.

No system is infallible. Access security also depends on each user safeguarding their credentials.

10. Individuals' rights

If your data is in a Client's instance —because you are an internal user at a company that uses PivetTrack, or a buyer on its B2B Portal—, that company is the controller of your data: please direct your access, correction, deletion or other requests to it. If you write to us directly, we will forward your request to the relevant Client and assist it with whatever it asks of us in order to respond.

Please note that, by design, movement and audit records are neither modified nor deleted, and accounts are deactivated rather than deleted; as a result, deleting some data may not be possible while the instance exists.

If we process your data as a controller (website, contact or business relationship), you may ask us for access, correction or deletion by writing to the address in Section 13. We respond within forty-five (45) days and, to protect your data, we verify your identity through the same email address we have on record.

11. Security incidents

If we confirm a security breach in the infrastructure under our control that exposes a Client's data, we will notify the Client within no more than seventy-two (72) hours of confirmation, in accordance with the Service Level Agreement. As the controller of that data, the Client decides on notifying the affected individuals, with the assistance it reasonably requests from us.

12. Minors

PivetTrack and its website are aimed at businesses, not at minors. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under eighteen (18) years of age.
