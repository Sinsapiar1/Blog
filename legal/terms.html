4. Product scope: what it does and what it does not

PivetTrack is a WMS. It includes the modules for Supplier Receipts, Customer Shipments, Customer and Supplier Returns, Shrinkage, Transfers between Warehouses, Inventory Counts (partial and full), the Kardex ledger, Weighted Average Cost (WAC) calculation, reports with Excel export, PDF documents branded with the Client's logo, and configuration of warehouses, suppliers, customers and the product catalog; as well as lot and expiration-date control with FEFO picking, lot traceability for recalls, a report of products nearing expiration, two-step receiving with blind verification, and receiving discrepancies with a double check and their resolution (including the credit note). As optional modules, not part of the Standard Plan and quoted separately, it offers the B2B Ordering Portal, for the Client's buyers to send it orders, and the B2B API, for their systems to integrate.

Traceability and compliance. PivetTrack records the lot, the expiration date and the destination of each movement from the data the Client enters, and makes it possible to reconstruct which customers received a lot. These tools support the Client's traceability and product recall processes, but they do not replace its regulatory obligations (for example, those of the FDA). Compliance with those rules, and the accuracy of the recorded lots and expiration dates, are the Client's responsibility.

PivetTrack is not, and does not replace:

  • an invoicing system: it issues no invoices or tax documents of its own. The PDF documents it generates are operational documents. With the QuickBooks Online integration, accounting documents are created in the Client's QuickBooks account, not in PivetTrack;
  • a purchasing system: it does not manage purchase orders or purchase approvals. It records the receipt of goods and, for reference, the supplier's document number;
  • an accounting system: it keeps no books of account and produces no financial statements. WAC and inventory valuation are calculated from the data the Client enters, for its information;
  • a payroll or human resources system;
  • a means of collecting or making payments.

Also not included in the standard deployment —and quoted separately if the Client asks for them—: the B2B Ordering Portal and the B2B API; custom development; integrations with third-party systems other than QuickBooks Online (for example, QuickBooks Desktop or another ERP); a database replica with automatic failover; point-in-time recovery (PITR); and the dedicated infrastructure of the Enterprise Plan.

QuickBooks Online. If the Client connects its QuickBooks Online company, that account belongs to the Client and is managed by the Client or its accountant, under the terms the Client agreed with Intuit. PivetTrack acts only as an automated bridge that creates in it the documents for confirmed operations, to avoid duplicate manual entry. It does not modify or delete existing records and does not provide accounting services. The Client reviews the recorded entries and decides their accounting and tax treatment. Pivet is not liable for the availability of QuickBooks or for changes Intuit makes to its service. The detailed roles are in the SOW (Section 2.4).
