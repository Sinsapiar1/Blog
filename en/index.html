The journey of a lot

  1. RECEIVEDRECIBIDO

    60 cases come through the door.

    Each one is logged with its lot and expiry date, against the supplier's document. If cases are missing, the difference is on record.

  2. AISLE 4-BPASILLO 4-B

    The system knows which aisle it's in.

    Lot by lot, location by location. Nobody has to walk the warehouse looking for it.

  3. SHIPPEDDESPACHADO

    What expires first ships first.

    52 cases go out to 4 customers. Each shipment stays tied to its customer, its lot and its date.

  4. RECALLRETIRO

    Then one day you have to pull it back.

    One click and you have the list: who received it, how many cases, and how many are still in your warehouse.
