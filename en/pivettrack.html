The journey of a lot

  1. It reaches the dock

    The office enters the invoice and the floor declares lots, expirations and quantities without seeing what was expected. If 120 cases were invoiced and 114 counted, another administrator verifies the difference and closes it with a credit, a replacement or a write-off.

  2. It goes to its aisle

    On receiving, everything is put away to its aisle. Stock is tracked by location, every move leaves a record and each aisle can be blind-recounted.

  3. The earliest-expiring lot ships first

    The picker scans the item or its case (EAN, UPC or ITF) with a phone or a handheld scanner. A customer shipment never takes an expired lot, and skipping the scan requires an administrator’s co-signature.

  4. It lands in your QuickBooks on its own

    Every shipment becomes an Invoice, every receipt a Bill, every customer return a Credit Memo and every supplier return a Vendor Credit. The account stays yours, managed by your accountant.

  5. If it comes back, it comes back at its cost

    You hold 200 units at an average cost of $12.00 and a customer returns 100 that shipped at $10.00. Restocking at today’s cost would leave $200 of phantom inventory. PivetTrack restocks at $10.00: 300 units, $3,400, average cost of $11.33.

  6. And if there’s a recall, you know who to call

    One click and you have the list of customers who received the lot, shipment by shipment and net of returns, ready for Excel.

    PivetTrack records traceability from the data your team enters and supports your recall processes. It does not replace your obligations to the FDA or under other health regulations.

Worth asking before you switch systems.

If your question isn’t here, we’ll cover it in the diagnostic session.

How long until it’s running?

It takes 2 to 3 weeks. We load your catalog from an Excel file or an export from your current system, and your team gets two 2-hour training sessions.

I already have QuickBooks. Why would I need this?

QuickBooks keeps your books, but it doesn’t know which lot is on which shelf or who it shipped to. PivetTrack runs the floor and sends QuickBooks what already happened, with no double entry. The QuickBooks Online integration is included. If you use Desktop, PivetTrack works the same without the integration, and connecting it is quoted separately.

Who touches my books?

Nobody on our side. During setup we connect your company and choose the accounts with your accountant. After that, PivetTrack only creates new documents and never changes or deletes what’s already in QuickBooks.

Can it handle my volume?

We measured it: with 165,382 SKUs, a 167,382-line full count closed in 229 seconds while 20 operators kept shipping, with 0 failed requests out of 6,176. Those are runs in a test environment, not a guarantee. Your SLA is the one in the contract.

How does it help with FSMA 204 traceability?

PivetTrack records the lot, expiration and destination of every movement, and builds a recall list in one click. That supports your traceability processes. Complying with the rule remains your company’s responsibility, and we won’t promise it.

What if I want to leave later?

During the contract you can export to Excel at any time: Current Stock, the ledger (Kardex), items near expiry, lot traceability and the count receipt. When the contract ends, if your account is paid up, your data stays available for export for 30 days. What’s already in QuickBooks stays in your QuickBooks.

Is my data safe?

Your company gets its own instance and its own database, shared with no one, with two-factor authentication for administrators, daily backups and a contractual 99.9% uptime commitment.
